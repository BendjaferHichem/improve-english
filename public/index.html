How it works

1

Talk naturally.

Tap the microphone and answer like you would to a friend. Maya asks follow-up questions, shares opinions, and keeps the conversation moving. You do most of the talking.

2

Get corrected naturally.

Small slips are let go. Useful fixes arrive in one friendly sentence, the way a person would say them, and never in a way that kills the flow.

3

Track your progress.

After each chat you get a short review: what to fix, expressions worth stealing, and the patterns that keep coming back. Future conversations quietly practise them.

Your voice stays yours.

We store transcripts, corrections and progress, never audio recordings. You can delete your history at any time. Speech recognition runs through your browser's built-in service; Chrome and Edge send audio to their own speech servers to do it.
